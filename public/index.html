Model a reallocation

Use current-period channel CPA as a starting point or enter your own assumptions. Adjust planned spend and expected CPA increase to explore diminishing efficiency.

Conversions = planned spend ÷ [reference CPA × (1 + assumed CPA increase)]. No response curve is estimated. These are editable scenarios, not optimized allocations or guaranteed outcomes. Reference CPA requires positive observed conversions.

Explore a randomized holdout result

Use assigned users and unique converters from two non-overlapping groups. Both groups need the same outcome window; analyze by original assignment.

A simple independent two-proportion normal approximation is shown only with at least 10 converters and 10 non-converters in both groups. It is not suitable for geo clusters, repeated users, sequential peeking, or non-random comparisons. This tool does not calculate required sample size.

About this app +

Searchlight Campaign Studio

The idea

The idea is to connect campaign planning, creative, economics, and measurement around one shared campaign context, instead of treating each decision as a separate exercise.

The problem it addresses

Campaign decisions become disconnected when planning, performance assumptions, and learning are handled separately.

Who it helps

Marketers developing campaign ideas, creative, and measurement plans.

What you can do

Brings eight campaign workspaces together with shared context, transparent calculations, and importable and exportable planning data.

The intended outcome

Connect campaign planning, creative, economics, and measurement in a shared workspace.
