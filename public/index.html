Explore a randomized holdout result

Use assigned users and unique converters from two non-overlapping groups. Both groups need the same outcome window; analyze by original assignment.

A simple independent two-proportion normal approximation is shown only with at least 10 converters and 10 non-converters in both groups. It is not suitable for geo clusters, repeated users, sequential peeking, or non-random comparisons. This tool does not calculate required sample size.
