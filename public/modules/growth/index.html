Paying subscribers
(Spend ÷ CPC) × trial conversion × paid conversion

Media CAC
Ad spend ÷ new paying subscribers

Contribution LTV
Monthly price × contribution margin ÷ monthly churn

Contribution in month m
New subscribers × (1 − churn)m − 1 × price × margin

Payback is the first whole billing month when cumulative cohort contribution covers ad spend. LTV estimates an undiscounted infinite lifetime with constant churn, price, and margin. It is not a revenue forecast. LTV/CAC is not a universal pass/fail benchmark.

This model excludes salaries, creative and agency costs, fixed overhead, refunds, taxes, annual plans, reactivation, trial delays, organic acquisition, and attribution effects. It assumes every counted paid subscriber is attributable to this spend; it does not establish incrementality. It does not model ongoing acquisition or diminishing returns as budget grows.
