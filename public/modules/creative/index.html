Formulas and interpretation

CTR = clicks / impressions. CPC = spend / clicks. Click-to-trial = trials / clicks. Trial-to-paid = paid / trials. Media CAC = spend / paid subscribers. Zero denominators display “Unavailable”.

The review cue prioritizes the lowest observed CAC only when all three concepts have positive spend and meet your chosen paid-conversion floor. It does not calculate statistical significance, incremental conversions, lifetime value, retention, or subscriber quality. More clicks alone do not identify a business winner.
