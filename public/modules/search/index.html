Campaign setup

Your campaign brief

First column: keyword. Optional header: keyword. Other columns are ignored. Imports are read in your browser. Selecting Generate with AI sends the product brief and up to 50 keywords to Gemini via the server.

How this prototype works

Generate with AI uses Gemini through a server endpoint. Build rule-based draft and Reset example work without AI. The sample rules cover subscription search intent; AI drafts also require review. No live keyword metrics are fetched.

Your planning workspace

Before launch

Validate the offer and landing page. Review keyword match behavior, exclusions, geography, budget, and conversion tracking in the advertising platform. Measure paid subscriptions as well as trials.

What would make this a good experiment?

Write a hypothesis for each group. Choose a primary conversion, a spending limit, and a review date before launch. Monitor search terms and customer quality. Cheap clicks alone do not establish profitable growth.
